Text in images

On manga and webtoon sites, click a speech bubble to read and translate its text. The text is recognised on your computer.

Permission
Sites
    Downloaded models

    Firefox asks for access to all websites because extensions may only capture the visible part of a tab without a click with that permission. Screenshots are taken only on the sites above, and never saved or sent.

    YouTube

    Turn on subtitles in the player, then click any word. Shift+click a second word to look up a phrase.

    Saved cards use the subtitle line as the sentence and link to the video at that moment.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Alt+Shift+LLook up the selection
    Alt+Shift+ALook up and add to Anki right away (with Anki on)
    EnterAdd to Anki (card open, with Anki on)
    PPlay pronunciation (card open)
    EEdit fields (card open)
    BUse the base form (card open)
    TShow the sentence translation, if hidden (card open)
    BackspaceBack to the speech bubble (manga)
    MShow more or less: all definitions, kanji, examples (card open)
    EscClose the card

    Change the global shortcuts in about:addons → ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts.
