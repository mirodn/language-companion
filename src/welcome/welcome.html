Welcome to Language Companion

Four short steps, and you can look up words on any page and save them to Anki.

1 Your languages

Lookups are offered only for text in these languages. You can change them later in the settings.

No Anki yet? You can still look up words. Words you save are kept and added once Anki is connected.

3 Decks

Each language's words go to its own deck.

Connect Anki first to choose decks.

4 Try it

Select a word below and click the small 文A button that appears.
