# Word frequency lists

The 20,000 most common words per language, most frequent first, one per line. Used for the
"Top 1k" … "Top 20k" badge on the lookup card (English, Korean and Chinese; Japanese uses JLPT levels from Jisho).

Built by `scripts/build-frequency.mjs` from
[FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave (OpenSubtitles 2018 word counts),
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: lowercased,
numbers and punctuation removed, counts removed, cut to 20,000 words. These lists are shared under
the same license.
