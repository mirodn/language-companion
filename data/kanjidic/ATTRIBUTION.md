# KANJIDIC2

English meanings and readings of kanji, for the kanji breakdown of Japanese words.

From [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project) by the
[Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Built by `scripts/build-kanjidic.mjs`.
Changes: only kanji with English meanings, at most three meanings and three on/kun readings each,
saved as tab-separated text. This data is shared under the same license.
